Bin day is one of those things everyone gets told eventually, usually by way of a missed collection or a neighbour's reminder - it's worth just looking it up in your first week instead.

## Why it's address-specific, not borough-wide

Collection days are set round by round, so two streets a few minutes apart in the same borough can easily have different days, and the same council can even run different schedules for houses versus flats on the same street. There's no single "bin day for this borough" - it has to be looked up by your specific address, usually through your council's own postcode-lookup tool.

## What goes in which bin varies too

Recycling rules aren't nationally standardised - what counts as recyclable, whether it's sorted into separate bins or one mixed bin, and what happens to food waste all differ by council. Assuming your new council follows the same rules as your old one is a common way to end up with a rejected collection, so it's worth checking the specific guidance for your new address rather than carrying over habits from before.

## Garden and bulky waste

Garden waste collection is often a separate, sometimes paid-for, opt-in service rather than something that's just included - worth checking if you have a garden and were expecting it to be automatic. Bulky waste (furniture, appliances, anything too large for a normal bin) usually needs a separate booked collection, sometimes with a fee, rather than being left out for the regular round.

## If a collection is missed

Most councils have a specific "report a missed collection" form rather than a general contact form, and using it directly is usually faster than a phone call - there's often also a window (commonly within one or two working days) within which you need to report it for a catch-up collection to be arranged.

## First week, not first month

Because the wrong bin on the wrong day is one of the more visible small mistakes you can make as a new resident, and because the answer is a two-minute postcode lookup, it's worth sorting this out as one of the first things you do rather than working it out by trial and error.
